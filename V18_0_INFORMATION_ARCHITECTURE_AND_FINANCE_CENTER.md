# V18.0 Information Architecture & Finance Center

## Dashboard
- Money-related KPI cards removed from the dashboard presentation.
- Dashboard is now reserved for operational volume: lessons, hours, students, teachers, makeups and changes.

## Finance Center
- Company Finance
- Teacher KPI
- Monthly Settlement

All three use their original calculation and rendering functions, but are presented inside one finance workspace.

## Progressive disclosure
- Expense, payroll and breakdown cards are collapsible.
- Teacher KPI supports search and sorting.
- A floating quick-action menu provides direct creation shortcuts.

## Compatibility
- Existing element IDs and business logic were preserved.
- Existing settlement links redirect into the Finance Center settlement tab.
