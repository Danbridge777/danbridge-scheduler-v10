# V17.24 Teacher Schedule Revenue Counting

## Final rule
- Revenue is counted from each teacher schedule assignment, not from unique class records.
- Base entry revenue = student hourly rate × scheduled duration.
- If one SC lesson is assigned to N teachers, company revenue receives N full entries.
- Payment status, collection confirmation, lesson report and attendance status do not change revenue.
- Draft lessons remain excluded.

## Examples
- SC, NT$1,000/hour, 2 hours, 3 teachers → NT$6,000 company timetable revenue.
- Tutoring, NT$800/hour, 1.5 hours, 1 teacher → NT$1,200 company timetable revenue.
